## 四、模拟真题（基于考点还原）

### 选择题（选出不正确的选项）

**第 1 题**：下列句子中，语法使用**不正确**的是（ ）
A. She has been living in Shenzhen since 2020.
B. If I were you, I would study harder.
C. The book which I bought it yesterday is very interesting.
D. Neither he nor I am going to the party.

> C 错误，定语从句中不需要 "it"

---

**第 2 题**：下列句子中，词语搭配**不正确**的是（ ）
A. look forward to meeting you
B. be used to get up early
C. succeed in passing the exam
D. insist on doing homework first

> B 错误，应为 "be used to getting up early"

---

**第 3 题**：下列句子中，时态使用**不正确**的是（ ）
A. By the time I arrived, the movie had already started.
B. She will have finished the project by next Friday.
C. We are playing football every Sunday.
D. He was reading when the phone rang.

> C 错误，表示经常性动作应用一般现在时："We play football every Sunday."

### 语法填空（部分考查形式）

**第 5 题**：阅读下面短文，用括号中所给词的适当形式填空。

The number of visitors to Shenzhen ________ (increase) rapidly in recent years. Many of them come here ________ (enjoy) the beautiful scenery and modern city life. Shenzhen, ________ (know) as "China's Silicon Valley," has become one of the ________ (important) tech cities in the world.

> has increased; to enjoy; known; most important

---

## 五、备考建议

1. 深高级英语**难度偏大**，需要有超出中考的英语水平
2. **"选不正确项"**的题型需要特别注意，每个选项都要仔细判断
3. 重点复习：
   - **复杂时态**（完成时、完成进行时）
   - **从句**（定语从句、状语从句、名词性从句）
   - **非谓语动词**（to do / doing / done 的区别）
4. 扩大词汇量，特别是**高频易混词汇**
5. 多做阅读理解训练，提高快速阅读和信息筛选能力
